The Story

Nonlinear by design.
Connected by conviction.

My path to tech was anything but straight. I came to the US as an immigrant, ran restaurants, earned a degree in occupational safety, then quit it all to launch a startup. That bet changed everything.

Since then I've co-founded two companies, built communities that grew into movements, written a book on the power of networks, and led product and marketing at high-growth startups. The thread connecting it all: a belief that the right people, in the right rooms, with the right resources, can do extraordinary things.

Today I live in Washington, DC with my wife Amanda and our dog MJ. Outside of work: tennis, live shows, movies, and an embarrassing number of podcast subscriptions.
