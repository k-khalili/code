My Story

A life lived in curiosity.

I grew up in a small coastal town where I spent more time taking apart old computers than playing outside. That curiosity turned into a career when I wrote my first line of code at 14 and realized I could make things out of nothing.

After studying Computer Science at State University, I moved to the city and started working on products used by millions of people. I've worn a lot of hats — engineer, founder, mentor — but the thread that connects them all is a love for solving hard problems with elegant solutions.

Outside of work I'm into photography, distance running, and convincing people that pineapple belongs on pizza.
