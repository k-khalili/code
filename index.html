The Story

Curiosity before credentials.
Results before recognition.

I grew up in a small coastal town where I spent more time taking apart old computers than playing outside. That curiosity turned into a career when I wrote my first line of code at 14 and realized I could make things out of nothing.

After studying Computer Science at State University, I moved to the city and started working on products used by millions of people. I’ve worn a lot of hats — engineer, founder, mentor — but the thread connecting them is a commitment to solving hard problems with elegance and intention.

Outside of work: photography, distance running, and a deeply held conviction that pineapple belongs on pizza.
